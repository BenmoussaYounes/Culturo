# Culturo — The Cultural Quiz App
<img width="6400" height="4800" alt="Mockup 4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d8cec8c9-3bbd-4ea5-9f27-3606d46824e2" />

Culturo is a mobile quiz app that challenges you to discover how well you really know the world — its history, geography, music, food, sports, and more. Whether you're playing solo, or climbing the global leaderboard, Culturo turns cultural knowledge into a daily game.

> *"Do you really know your country?"*

---

## What You Can Do

- **Play solo quizzes** across countries and cultural categories — history, art, sports, science, geography, and more
- **Earn XP and level up** as you answer questions and complete sessions
- **Climb the leaderboard** — globally, regionally, or among friends
- **Take on daily and monthly challenges** for bonus rewards
- **Track your progress** with a personal profile showing your level, XP, and battle record
